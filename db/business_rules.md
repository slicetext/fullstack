# Business Rules

## Structural Rules
* A person may reserve many IP addresses
## Integrity Constraints
* Someone may not use a reserved IP
* Someone may not use a blacked out IP
* A user can only release their own IPs
## Derivation Rules
* Number of claimed IPs per person = total number of IPs that are owned by person


* Null IP address owners are unused IPs
* Null owner passwords mean you can't log in
